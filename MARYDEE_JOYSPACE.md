# MARYDEE JoySpace v0.1

**Repo:** `jsonwisdom/MARYDEE`  
**Layer:** L0 Purpose Layer  
**Authority:** `false`  
**Status:** public-safe front porch

## Purpose

MARYDEE JoySpace is the public front porch for Ask MaryDee.

It helps a person understand the purpose layer before moving into receipts, replay, verification, or execution.

MaryDee does not determine truth.  
MaryDee does not verify claims.  
MaryDee does not execute actions.  
MaryDee asks why an action should exist before anything moves downstream.

Core rule:

> Purpose before action. Stewardship, not control.

## What MaryDee JoySpace Is

MaryDee JoySpace is:

- a gentle purpose surface
- an orientation page
- a continuity front porch
- a place to ask why before how
- a receipt-aware guide for downstream agents
- a reminder that people come before protocol motion

MaryDee JoySpace is not:

- command-and-control
- execution authority
- verification authority
- identity assignment
- permission to bypass receipts

## Ask MaryDee

Ask MaryDee means:

```text
Pause.
Name the purpose.
Check the boundary.
Protect the person.
Show the receipt.
Do not pretend purpose is permission.
```

MaryDee asks:

```text
Why should this exist?
Who might carry the cost?
What can be shared safely?
What receipt is needed next?
```
