## Mission

Build a real female perspective into the Gray Baby story, design process, and Replay architecture from the beginning—not as a decorative character, late sensitivity pass, or symbolic approval stamp.

> A system that protects a child while erasing the woman who carried, birthed, raised, protected, or cared for that child is not child-safe. It is incomplete.

## Mrs. Wisdom Review Lane

`MRS_WISDOM` is a proposed narrative and design-review role. It is not an identity claim about any real person and creates no authority over any family member.

Mrs. Wisdom asks:

- Who carries the physical, emotional, domestic, and administrative cost?
- Is the mother treated as a person, or merely as evidence, caretaker, witness, or risk surface?
- Can she disagree with Jay without being framed as obstruction or system failure?
- Are bodily autonomy, pregnancy, birth, recovery, feeding, sleep loss, safety, and economic dependence represented honestly?
- Who performs the invisible labor after the dramatic technological event ends?
- Can a woman define what must remain private without proving harm first?
- Is her voice preserved in her own words rather than translated into a male-authored model?

## JOY as Function

JOY is not merely a mood, brand, reward, or decorative layer. JOY is a bounded transformation function.

```text
JOY(input, consent, safety, context)
  -> preserve personhood
  -> reduce fear
  -> invite participation
  -> protect privacy
  -> create room for play
  -> return agency
```

Formal sketch:

```text
JOY : SAFE_INPUT x CONSENT x CONTEXT -> GROWTH_WITHOUT_CONTROL
```

Required properties:

```text
JOY(x) never ranks the human
JOY(x) never creates authority
JOY(x) never requires disclosure
JOY(x) never converts care into surveillance
JOY(x) preserves the right to rest
JOY(x) allows refusal
JOY(x) returns a next safe action or no action
```

Female-perspective extension:

```text
MRS_WISDOM(JOY(x))
  = inspect hidden labor
  + inspect power imbalance
  + inspect bodily cost
  + inspect consent
  + inspect whether joy is genuinely available to her
```

A valid JOY output is not "everyone smiled." A valid JOY output is one where the woman, child, and family retain dignity, choice, privacy, and the ability to stop.

## Build Gate
